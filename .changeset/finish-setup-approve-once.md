The Shipments page offers **Finish setup** when the store is already connected to DinkusKit: one press by an Administrator, no second approval at DinkusKit.com, and Ship turns on. The orders page says which business the store is connected to and which DinkusKit plugins share it, and uninstalling Ship cancels only its own DinkusKit key. A store whose Ship is off at DinkusKit.com is asked to press Connect again, which turns it on. Needs DinkusKit.com with the Finish setup routes; until then the page shows Connect as before.
