**Can I buy live USPS postage?**

Not yet. The hosted service runs in sandbox mode. Sandbox and practice labels are not valid postage; do not use them to mail a parcel.

**Do I need Commerce?**

Yes. The current connection flow requires Commerce first, and Ship receives orders from it. Installing Ship does not build a storefront.

**Does cancelling a label refund the customer's order?**

No. Label cancellation, customer payment refunds and order cancellation are separate operations.

**Does Ship manage stock?**

No. Ship handles labels and reports tracking to Commerce; physical stock belongs to an inventory system.

Read the [Ship guide](https://dinkuskit.com/docs/reference/ship), [browse the source](https://github.com/dinkuskit/ship), or [report an issue](https://github.com/dinkuskit/ship/issues).
