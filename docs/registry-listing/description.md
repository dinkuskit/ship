DinkusKit Ship brings shipping-label work into your EmDash admin.

Choose a Commerce order, review its package and USPS rates, then create, print or cancel its test label. The hosted Ship service keeps the label records and reports tracking back to Commerce.

**Sandbox postage only.** Live postage is not enabled. Sandbox and practice labels are not valid postage and cannot be used to mail a parcel.

Commerce must be connected first. Ship is optional: Commerce can also record manual fulfillment and tracking without it. Ship does not manage product inventory or take customer payments.
